About Me

I'm a Senior Front-End Developer with 5+ years of experience building and maintaining scalable web applications, enterprise platforms and multilingual digital products.

I currently work at AlmavivA S.p.A. on Italian government platforms, including the public health platform FSE 2.0, a national fishing platform, maps platforms and a notifications platform. I build complex citizen-facing workflows, integrate multiple public-sector APIs, and improve architecture, accessibility and code quality.

  • Accessibility (a11y) & responsive UI
  • Unit testing with Jasmine & Karma
  • SonarQube & clean code practices
